# Vehicle Service & Maintenance

Implemented per-vehicle history, paginated newest-first records, latest/next service and recorded-cost summaries, add form, details, confirmed deletion, multiple gallery/camera bills, previews, removal before saving, and saved-bill viewing. Uses the existing black/emerald theme, Expo Router stack, SQLite and app-private storage. No new dependencies.

## Data and storage

- Migration **4 → 5** only adds `vehicle_services`, `service_bill_photos`, `service_bill_cleanup`, indexes and a deletion trigger. Existing vehicles, gallery metadata and other tables are preserved. Fresh installations run all versioned migrations.
- Services reference vehicles with `ON DELETE CASCADE`. Bills have a composite `(vehicle_id, service_id)` foreign key, preventing a bill from belonging to a different vehicle than its service.
- Optional information, including unentered costs, is nullable. Explicit zero is preserved. Totals are derived, never independently stored. If all costs are blank, the total is `Not added`; otherwise missing components contribute zero. Amounts accept up to two decimals and a maximum of ₹1 billion per component. Odometers must be finite and non-negative. Dates use validated `YYYY-MM-DD` text.
- The history index orders `(vehicle_id, service_date DESC, created_at DESC, id DESC)`. History loads 40 records per page with a stable cursor and renders a FlatList without bill images. Next Service comes from the latest service record. Total Maintenance Cost sums entered costs and identifies records without costs.
- Bills live in `Paths.document/vehicle-photos/{vehicleId}/service-bills/{serviceId}/{photoId}.{extension}`. SQLite holds metadata only. The existing normal gallery table and storage validator are unchanged.
- File access validates IDs and URI segments, accepts valid encoded scope names, rejects traversal/encoded separators, and rebases only an exact owned suffix. Recursive cleanup constructs its target from validated IDs, never from a metadata URI.
- Creation records cleanup intent before asynchronous copies. The service, bill rows and removal of cleanup intent commit together on a private SQLite connection with foreign keys enabled before BEGIN. Failed or interrupted imports retain cleanup intent when needed.
- Deletion atomically removes the service and bill metadata and queues cleanup via a trigger. Filesystem failure does not restore a deleted record: a durable job remains. Cleanup refuses live services and uses the existing vehicle operation lock. History and Garage retry pending jobs; failures expose Retry cleanup. Vehicle deletion already removes the enclosing vehicle photo directory; its new service/bill relationships cascade and remaining service cleanup jobs are idempotent.
- Service odometers do not overwrite the vehicle's current odometer.

## Exact manual device checklist

1. Install the updated APK over the previous app **without uninstalling or clearing app data**. Open Garage; confirm existing vehicles, details, cover photos and normal galleries remain intact.
2. Open vehicle A → **Service & Maintenance**. With no services, confirm “No service records yet”, “Not added” summaries and **Add Service**. Use the visible Back arrow and Android Back to return to Vehicle Overview; reopen history.
3. Tap Add Service and save blank fields. Confirm required errors. Try odometer `-1`, next odometer `-1`, cost `-1`, cost `1.001`, and date `2026-02-30`. Confirm rejection. Correct them before saving.
4. Create `Oil and filter`, service date `2026-09-01`, odometer `12000`, parts `1200.50`, labour `350`, other `49.50`. Confirm live Total Cost is **₹1,600**. Add workshop, work performed, next date `2027-03-01`, next odometer `18000`, and notes.
5. Select multiple gallery bills. Preview one and close it with Android Back. Remove one selected bill. Take a camera photo; test permission denial first if possible, then grant access and retry. Save once; confirm one service is created and only retained bills appear in details.
6. Open details and check every field and total. Open each saved bill; test Close bill and Android Back. Restart the app, reopen the service and verify persistence and image availability, including with airplane mode enabled.
7. Add an older service dated `2026-08-01`, entering only required fields. Verify it appears below September, optional details/costs show “Not added”, the September next-service summary remains, and the total remains ₹1,600. Add a newer service with explicit zero cost; confirm zero displays distinctly and next-service information follows that newest record.
8. Open vehicle B. Confirm A's services/bills are absent. Add B's service with a bill. Check both normal vehicle galleries: service bills must not appear there.
9. On A's service details, tap Delete Service → Cancel; verify nothing changes. Repeat and confirm deletion. Verify the record disappears, summary updates, A remains, A's normal gallery remains, B's service/bills remain and Personal Expense Manager is unchanged. Original source photos in the phone gallery should remain.
10. Test visible and Android Back from history, Add Service, details, and the bill modal. Leave Add Service during a save of several large bills: it must not unexpectedly navigate you back when the save finishes. Return to history after completion to inspect the result.
11. With disposable test data, add more than 40 services and scroll through history. Confirm pagination has no duplicates and remains responsive.
12. If a controlled low-storage or filesystem failure can be reproduced safely on a test device, verify failed imports create no partial record; pending cleanup survives restart. For deletion cleanup failures, the service should remain deleted and Retry cleanup should finish after storage recovers. Do not alter production files to simulate this.
13. Optional regression with a disposable vehicle: add normal photos and service bills, then use the existing confirmed vehicle deletion. Confirm that vehicle's records/files are removed and other vehicles remain unaffected.
